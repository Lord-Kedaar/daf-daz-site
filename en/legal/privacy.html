As of: 01.01.2026

1. Controller

Radoslaw Pleskot
Vienna, Austria
Email: kontakt@radoslaw-pleskot.com

2. Data collection

2.1 Website access

When visiting this website, the following data may be processed:

  • IP address (anonymized or truncated where technically possible)
  • date and time of access
  • browser type and version
  • operating system
  • visited pages
  • duration of visit

2.2 Contact

When contacting me via contact form or email, the following data is processed:

  • name
  • email address
  • phone number (optional)
  • message content

The language of the website is determined by the URL you visit (separate URLs for German, Polish and English). The selected appearance (light/dark theme) is stored locally in your browser to display the website accordingly.

3. Legal basis

Processing is based on:

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – processing inquiries
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest in a secure and functional website

4. Data retention

Personal data is stored only as long as necessary for the stated purposes or required by law.

5. Third-party services – Contact form

This website uses Formspree to process contact requests.

Data transfers to third countries are carried out on the basis of Standard Contractual Clauses pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR.

More information: https://formspree.io/privacy

6. Cookies and local settings

This website does not use cookies. It only stores the selected appearance (light/dark theme) locally in your browser. The language is determined by the URL you visit.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and objection in accordance with GDPR.

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary. The current version is always available on this website.
